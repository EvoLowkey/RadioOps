Workplace guide

Employee Help

Using your radio

  1. Account accessVerify your email, sign in, and wait for Manager approval when joining.
  2. Check outChoose an Available radio or scan its QR code, then confirm Check Out.
  3. ReturnTap Scan QR to Return and scan the exact radio assigned to you. Camera access is required.
  4. PasswordUse Forgot password? on the sign-in page if you cannot remember your password.
  5. CameraUse Safari on iPhone/iPad or Chrome on Android and allow camera access when prompted.

Manager Help

Managing the operation

  1. EmployeesApprove new accounts, disable access, promote Managers, or remove former employees.
  2. Radio conditionOpen a radio and mark it Lost, Damaged, In Repair, or Available with a reason.
  3. Lost radiosA Lost radio can retain its open assignment so accountability stays connected to the last employee.
  4. OverridesUse Manager return controls when a QR label is damaged or operational recovery is required.
  5. AuditReview protected system events for account and fleet changes.

Support
